A Guide to Wielding the Force

Purpose:

This site will provide information relating to combat among force users as we see in the Star Wars universe. It will include information on powerful force users, types of kyber crystals, and the most common fighting styles used.

Scenarios:

What are the most common, rarest, and most powerful types of kyber crystals?

What fighting style is preferred for which force user? Which styles are good and bad matchups?

Who are the top force users that are known? What fighting style did they use, when did the live, and what did they accomplish?
